02 / HighlightsWhat you get

What's inside.

01

Read your way

Tabs, four page layouts, Read mode, thumbnails, outline and your own bookmarks, plus read aloud with the voices in Windows.

02

Find anything

Search with a results list and the words around each match, or search every PDF in a folder at once.

03

Edit and mark up

Change text in place, add text boxes, stamps, drawings and shapes, highlight, underline and comment.

04

Forms

Fill forms in place, create and edit fields, or turn a printed form into a fillable one automatically.

05

Sign

Visual signatures drawn, typed or from an image, or standard digital signatures with a certificate.

06

Protect and redact

Password-protect with AES-256, truly remove content with redaction, and strip hidden information.

07

Organize pages

Reorder, crop, split, extract and insert pages; add watermarks, page numbers, headers, footers and Bates numbers.

08

Convert

Word (as-is or structured), PowerPoint, Excel, web page, Markdown, PDF/A, text and images.

09

On-device OCR

Make scanned PDFs searchable and copyable with the OCR engine built into Windows.

10

Batch processing

Run OCR, watermarks, redaction and more on many PDFs at once. Originals are never changed.

11

Compare and check

Compare two PDFs word by word, and check accessibility with one-click fixes.

12

Print and compress

Sharp vector printing with comments and form values, and smaller copies with Compress.

Requirements
OSWindows 10 1809 or later, Windows 11
Architecturex64, x86, ARM64
OCRA Windows language with OCR (English on most PCs)
Read aloudThe voices built into Windows

Good to know: VisaryPDF works offline. It only goes online if you choose to add a signature timestamp or long-term validation information when signing with a certificate. Compare checks text only, not images or formatting, and the accessibility check cannot add tags to untagged PDFs.
