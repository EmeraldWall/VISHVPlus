About

Stays out of your way.

DesClock sits on your desktop and does one thing well. No taskbar clutter, no background services, no telemetry, and no AI integration.

🕑

Digital or analog

Switch between a digital time readout or a classic analog face with hour numbers, both showing the full date underneath.

🎨

Fully customizable

Pick your own colors, or follow your Windows theme automatically. Choose separate fonts for time and date, adjust opacity, and dial in a soft drop shadow.

📌

Flexible placement

Drag it anywhere on screen, or snap it to any of 9 screen positions — corners, edges, or center — from Settings.

🔍

Built for high-DPI

Renders crisp and sharp at any Windows display scaling. Never blurry, never pixelated.

🪟

Respects your system

Reads your Windows accent color and light/dark theme live — no restart needed. Optional start-with-Windows toggle.

🔒

Genuinely private

No network access, no data collection, no ads, and no AI integration. Full stop.

Getting Started

Up and running in a minute.

1

Install from the Store

Get DesClock from the Microsoft Store — it installs in seconds and appears on your desktop right away.

2

Open Settings

Right-click the clock, or double-click its system tray icon, to open the resizable Settings window.

3

Make it yours

Pick a face, choose colors and fonts, and snap it to the spot on screen where you want it to live.
