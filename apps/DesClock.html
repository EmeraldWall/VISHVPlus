02 / HighlightsWhat you get

What's inside.

01

Digital or analog

A digital time readout or a classic analog face with hour numbers, both showing the full date underneath.

02

Fully customizable

Pick your own colors or follow your Windows theme, choose separate fonts for time and date, and adjust opacity.

03

Soft drop shadow

Dial in a diffused shadow so the clock stays readable on any wallpaper.

04

Flexible placement

Drag it anywhere, or snap it to any of 9 screen positions: corners, edges or center.

05

Crisp on high-DPI

Renders sharp at any Windows display scaling, never blurry or pixelated.

06

Respects your system

Reads your accent color and light or dark theme live, with no restart. Optional start with Windows.
