DesClock

A tiny desktop clock widget with a digital or analog face, fully customizable, and light on RAM.

Microsoft Store Paid app 0.6 MB No telemetry

About DesClock

DesClock is a lightweight desktop clock widget that sits on your desktop and stays out of your way. No taskbar clutter, no background services, no telemetry, and no AI integration.

  • Digital or analog: Switch between a digital time readout or a classic analog clock face with hour numbers, both showing the full date underneath.
  • Fully customizable: Pick your own colors for the clock (or let it follow your Windows light/dark theme automatically), choose separate fonts for the time and date, adjust opacity, and dial in a soft diffused drop shadow to taste.
  • Flexible placement: Drag it anywhere on screen or snap it to any of 9 screen positions (corners, edges, or center) from Settings.
  • Built for high-DPI displays: Renders crisp and sharp at any Windows display scaling, not blurry or pixelated.
  • Respects your system: Reads your Windows accent color and light/dark theme live, with no restart needed when you switch. Optional "start with Windows" toggle if you want it launching automatically at sign-in.
  • Genuinely private: No network access, no data collection, no ads, and no AI integration.

What's new

Initial release: digital/analog clock, custom colors and fonts, adjustable shadow and opacity, 9-position screen snapping, light/dark/system theming, and a modern, resizable Settings dialog.
