03 / PrinciplesShared by every app

Built to stay out of your way.

Every VISHVPlus app is held to the same short list of rules.

◯01

Private by default

No telemetry and no analytics. If an app ever needed to send data anywhere, its privacy section would say so plainly first.

▢02

Your files, your PC

Documents, settings and saved signatures stay on your device. There are no cloud uploads and no accounts to create.

≃03

Light by design

Small installs, low memory use, and nothing running in the background that you didn't start.

▦04

Made for Windows

Apps follow your Windows theme, stay sharp at any display scale and work with the keyboard.

✕05

Nothing extra

No ads, no upsells and no AI bolted on. Each app does its job and gets out of the way.

✓06

Real support

Every app has its own support page and privacy section, and every ticket is read.
