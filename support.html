Frequently asked.

01

Where can I download your apps?

All VISHVPlus apps are distributed exclusively through the Microsoft Store. See the Apps page for links.

02

I was charged for an app — where do refunds come from?

Purchases and refunds for Microsoft Store apps are handled by Microsoft, not VISHVPlus directly. Request a refund via your Microsoft order history. We're happy to help with anything else via a support ticket above.

03

Do I need an account to get support?

Tickets are filed through our issue tracker, which asks you to sign in with a free account before sending. That lets you follow your ticket and get notified when we reply.

04

Where is your Privacy Policy?

See our Privacy Policy page, which covers DesClock, VisaryPDF and all VISHVPlus apps.

05

Do the apps come with a warranty?

No. Our apps are provided as is, without warranty, under the license on their Microsoft Store listing and our Terms of Service. Consumer rights you have under local law are not affected.
