Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how VISHVPlus ("VISHVPlus", "we", "us", or "our") handles information in connection with the Windows applications we publish on the Microsoft Store, including DesClock and any future VISHVPlus apps that link to this policy (each, an "App").

This is a general policy that applies to all VISHVPlus Apps. Where an App collects or handles information differently, that is described in the app-specific section below. If an App has no dedicated section yet, the general sections of this policy apply to it.

1. Information Collected by the Microsoft Store

Our Apps are distributed exclusively through the Microsoft Store. When you browse, purchase, download, install, or update an App, Microsoft collects information (such as your Microsoft account details, payment information, device identifiers, and download/usage diagnostics) as described in the Microsoft Privacy Statement. VISHVPlus does not receive your payment card details — purchases are processed entirely by Microsoft.

2. Information Collected by Our Apps

Unless stated otherwise in the app-specific section below, VISHVPlus Apps:

  • Do not require you to create a VISHVPlus account.
  • Do not collect personal information such as your name, physical address, or contact list.
  • Store your app settings and preferences locally on your device.
  • Do not sell personal information to third parties.

Any exception to the above — such as use of analytics, crash reporting, or cloud sync — will be explicitly disclosed in the relevant app-specific section of this policy.

3. How We Use Information

Where an App does process information beyond what is described above (as disclosed in its app-specific section), we use it only to operate, maintain, and improve that App, and to provide support when you contact us.

4. Data Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties, except:

  • With Microsoft, as necessary to distribute and license the App through the Microsoft Store;
  • Where required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of VISHVPlus or others;
  • With service providers who process data strictly on our behalf, if disclosed in an app-specific section.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any information handled by our Apps. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

Our Apps are not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required by your local law), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us via the Support page so we can take appropriate action.

7. Your Choices

You can stop all information collection by an App simply by uninstalling it from your device. If an App offers in-app settings related to data or permissions, those are described in its app-specific section.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. Continued use of an App after a change becomes effective constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how a specific App handles your information, please reach out via our Support page, which opens a ticket on our public GitHub repository.

App-Specific Section: DesClock

DesClock is a lightweight desktop clock widget for Windows, distributed on the Microsoft Store (Product ID: 9N9BN0W920H2). In addition to the general terms above:

  • DesClock has no network access — it does not connect to the internet at all.
  • DesClock does not require account creation or sign-in.
  • DesClock stores your display preferences (clock face, colors, fonts, opacity, shadow, screen position, and theme choice) locally on your device only.
  • DesClock does not collect analytics, does not use AI, does not show ads, and does not transmit any data to VISHVPlus or anyone else.

This section reflects DesClock's current data practices. If a future update to DesClock adds network access, analytics, cloud sync, or any other data collection, this section will be updated accordingly before that update is released.
